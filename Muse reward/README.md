## About The Entry

**INFLUCIO** is an AI-driven platform designed to bridge brands and influencers more authentically and intelligently. We believe technology should drive efficiency while fostering deeper human connections.

Our core innovations include:
- **AI-Powered Campaign Creation**: Our proprietary engine analyzes product traits, audience trends, and influencer profiles to recommend optimized strategies.
- **Intelligent Influencer Matching**: Machine learning algorithms align interests, engagement metrics, and brand values.
- **ROI Estimation Models**: Forecasts reach and performance before campaigns launch.
- **Multi-Channel Activation**: Seamlessly coordinates Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and multi-platform workflows inside one unified dashboard.

---

## Design Director Leadership

As **Design Director**, Tongfang Sun led the design team through a user-centered, AI-driven product development process:
- Applied **design thinking** to uncover user needs by interviewing over **50 influencers across Asia, Europe, and America** across multiple social media platforms.
- Facilitated **cross-functional collaboration** with machine learning engineers and data scientists to co-create AI-powered features, ensuring UX research insights directly informed model behavior and interaction patterns.
- Partnered with marketing leadership to redefine business goals and align them with verified user value.
- Architected a **modular design system** and introduced the concept of **responsive AI** to deliver adaptive, personalized interfaces.

## Impact & Client Reach

What sets **INFLUCIO** apart is its human-centered, AI-enhanced UX. Through deep research with entrepreneurs and creators, the team crafted a seamless, intuitive interface emphasizing transparency, accessibility, and ease of use.

INFLUCIO empowers small businesses, entrepreneurs, artists, and startups by making influencer marketing accessible, scalable, and genuine.

- **Headquarters**: Silicon Valley, United States  
- **Client Roster**: Temu, Luma.ai, Shopline, Open Audio, Collov.ai, Brix.ai, and more.  
- **Scale**: **2.8M+ influencers** and billions of followers worldwide, setting a new standard for AI-powered, human-first brand engagement.
