## About The Entry

**INFLUCIO** is an AI-driven platform designed to bridge brands and influencers more authentically and intelligently. We believe technology should drive efficiency while fostering deeper human connections.

A college mentorship project supporting design school students with their entrepreneurship venture:
- **AI-Powered Campaign Creation**: Engine analyzes product traits, audience trends, and influencer profiles to recommend optimized strategies.
- **Intelligent Influencer Matching**: Machine learning algorithms align interests, engagement metrics, and brand values.
- **ROI Estimation Models**: Forecasts reach and performance before campaigns launch.
- **Multi-Channel Activation**: Seamlessly coordinates Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and multi-platform workflows inside one unified dashboard.

---

## Design Mentorship & Guidance

As **Design Mentor**, Tongfang Sun guided the student founding team through a user-centered, AI-driven product development process for their college entrepreneurship project:
- Mentored students in **qualitative user research** to uncover creator needs by interviewing over **50 influencers across Asia, Europe, and America** across multiple social media platforms.
- Facilitated **cross-functional collaboration** with machine learning engineers and data scientists to co-create AI-powered features, ensuring UX research insights directly informed model behavior and interaction patterns.
- Guided student founders on defining core product value propositions and translating early customer discovery into functional prototypes.
- Architected a **modular design system** and introduced the concept of **responsive AI** to deliver adaptive, personalized interfaces.
