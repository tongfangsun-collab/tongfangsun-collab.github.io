About

Hello! I’m Tong Fang Sun, a UX Designer & Creative Technologist focused on the intersection of minimalism, human-centered AI, and expressive interactive web experiences.

I believe great design strips away unnecessary complexity until only intuitive, joyful interaction remains — whether crafting a clean product workflow, folding a 6-step origami Pikachu with hand gestures, or strolling through The Metropolitan Museum of Art after hours.
