About

Hello! I’m Tong Fang Sun, a UX Designer & Creative Technologist focused on the intersection of human-centered AI, spatial gesture interfaces, and expressive interactive systems.

I believe great design strips away unnecessary friction until only intuitive, joyful interaction remains — whether crafting a precision product workflow, folding a 6-step origami Pikachu with hand gestures, or strolling through The Metropolitan Museum of Art after hours.
