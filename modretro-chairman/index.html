Chairman's Adventure

An Adventure Island / Super Mario-inspired side-scrolling platformer built natively in C (GBDK-2020) for the ModRetro Chromatic handheld — starring Chairman in his signature black quilted vest with red trim! Battle mischievous Cats & Mice, bump ? blocks to eat Treats (+HP), throw spinning Bone Boomerangs, and defeat all 3 bosses: Stage 1 Lion 🦁, Stage 2 Tiger 🐯, and Stage 3 Elephant 🐘.

How to Run on a Real ModRetro Chromatic

  1. Download chairman-adventure-rom.zip and unzip to get chairman-adventure.gbc (or download the raw .gbc directly).
  2. Via Flashcart (EverDrive-GB X7 / EZ-Flash Jr.): Copy chairman-adventure.gbc onto your FAT32 microSD card and boot on your ModRetro Chromatic.
  3. Via Dedicated GBC Cartridge: Flash chairman-adventure.gbc onto any 32 KB+ reflashable GBC cart via GBxCart RW or Joey Jr.
  4. Via Emulator: Open chairman-adventure.gbc in SameBoy, mGBA, BGB, OpenEmu, or Delta.
